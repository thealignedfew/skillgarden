How to use this page
  1. Each card under "Questions for you" is one open ask. Answer by commenting on the card and sending the comment to Claude. The comment wakes the session that owns this page.
  2. Each card says what happens if you say nothing. Silence is a valid answer.
  3. Copy puts the card's answer code, or the full text to paste somewhere else, on your clipboard.
  4. Each card carries an Eisenhower rating (Important, Urgent, both or neither), a stage and a most responsible party (MRP). The stage flow runs Open, Start, In review, Completed on the left; Blocked, Deferred and Superseded are parked on the right. Open means the party that raised the card owns it. Start, In review and Completed clicked here set the MRP to the reviewer with the time; type any other name (a session, a colleague, a blocking card) in the MRP box. All of it is saved with the page and the owning session reads it at the start of every turn, so a click is enough; comment only when there is something to say.
  5. Rank order is Important + urgent, then Urgent, then Important, then Neither, oldest card first within each. The bar above the cards filters by stage, quadrant and MRP, searches the text, and changes the sort. Completed and Superseded cards are hidden until you pick All stages.
  6. A card that asks about many items lists them in a table, each row with Approve or Strike. Your clicks are saved with the page and the session applies the approved rows; the answer line under the table updates as you go, so a comment is only needed for something the buttons cannot say.
  7. Comment on the card itself (its id, title or text). The session routes your comment by the card you highlighted, because cards can move when the page is republished.
  8. Once a card is answered, the session records your ruling, updates this page and closes the thread. Answered cards leave the page.
  9. If this session manages other seats, every question from the crew is a card on this one Parley tab, tagged with the seat that asked it; the manager relays your answer by the card's id. The Crew tab holds each awake seat's phase, what it has been doing and the state of its work. The Sleepers tab holds seats that are mothballed or idle with no loop armed: a seat that completed its mission rests there until the mission comes back.
  10. The Artifacts tab lists every page this session has published, live or retired.
  11. This page is the review surface. Keep your own record of decisions elsewhere.
