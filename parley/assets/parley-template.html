How to use this page
  1. Each card under "Questions for you" is one open ask. Type your answer in the box on the card and press Send answer: it marks the card Responded, hands it back to the session that asked, and sends your words to that session. A comment on the card, sent to Claude, still works the same way.
  2. Each card says what happens if you say nothing. Silence is a valid answer.
  3. The top of each card is what needs you now: the question, the live steps, the latest update and the answer box. Everything older (earlier updates, finished steps, your past comments, the background) sits under History and background at the bottom of the card, on a shaded panel. It opens by itself at the Exhaustive detail level and is hidden at Zero.
  4. Copy puts the card's answer code, or the full text to paste somewhere else, on your clipboard.
  5. Each card carries an Eisenhower rating (Important, Urgent, both or neither), a stage and a most responsible party (MRP). The stage flow runs Open, Responded, In review, Completed on the left; Blocked, Deferred and Superseded are parked on the right. Open means the party that raised the card owns it. Responded means you have answered and hands the card back to the session that asked; In review sets the MRP to the reviewer. Completed clicked here marks the card done for the session that asked to confirm: it checks the work, then closes the card or reopens it and says what is missing, so a click here cannot close a card early. Pin keeps a card at the top of the list; type any other name (a session, a colleague, a blocking card) in the MRP box. All of it is saved with the page and the session reads it at the start of every turn, so a click is enough; comment only when there is something to say.
  6. A click on a stage button, or on Important, Urgent or Pin, waits five seconds before it is saved. The card stays where it is, a thin bar runs down along its top edge, and Undo takes the click back. Several clicks on one card are saved together. Your typed answer and your rulings on item rows are saved at once.
  7. Rank order is Important + urgent, then Urgent, then Important, then Neither, oldest card first within each. The bar above the cards filters by stage, quadrant and MRP, searches the text, and changes the sort. Completed and Superseded cards are hidden until you pick All stages. Group sorts the cards into sections by topic, by the seat that asked, or both; the sections with the most important and urgent cards come first, and a click on a section's bar folds or opens it.
  8. A card that asks about many items lists them in a table, each row with Approve, Hold or Strike. Hold keeps a row for later: it is not applied and not dropped. Your clicks are saved with the page and the session applies the approved rows; the answer line under the table updates as you go, so a comment is only needed for something the buttons cannot say. Once a card is closed, each row shows its final ruling as a label.
  9. Comment on the card itself (its id, title or text). The session routes your comment by the card you highlighted, because cards can move when the page is republished.
  10. Once a card is answered, the session records your ruling, updates this page and closes the thread. Answered cards leave the page.
  11. If this session manages other seats, every question from the crew is a card on this one Parley tab, tagged with the seat that asked it; the manager relays your answer by the card's id. The Crew tab holds each awake seat's phase, what it has been doing and the state of its work. The Sleepers tab holds seats that are mothballed or idle with no loop armed: a seat that completed its mission rests there until the mission comes back.
  12. The Artifacts tab lists every page this session has published, live or retired.
  13. This page is the review surface. Keep your own record of decisions elsewhere.
