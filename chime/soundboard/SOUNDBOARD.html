    Clock

    Clock out and every session knows you are away. Chime goes quiet. Blocked and ignored counts stop, so no loop moves up to #StandReadyHigh and nothing tries to reach you. Questions wait until you clock back in.

    Channels

    M queues a mute. ▶ previews a generated tone in this page only; your own files play on your PC.

    questions

    This sound plays only when a lead session surfaces its reports' questions on two ticks in a row. It plays at most once per cooldown across all sessions, so several leads cannot chain it. Any tick inside the cooldown plays its usual sound.

    A decision waiting on you

    by who started it, below

    Who started the turn

    • You: ack when your prompt lands, done when it finishes.
    • Another session: peer.
    • A loop tick: #KeepWarm plays tick, #StandReady tick_ready, #StandReadyHigh tick_high, #StandReadyLow tick_low, #CompactionForm tick_compaction.
    • Anything else (other scheduled jobs, background tasks, continuations): nothing unless it needs you.

    When a seat cannot reach you

    • A blocked tick plays urgent three times.
    • After three blocked ticks the loop moves up to #StandReadyHigh, with escalate.
    • Four needy #StandReadyHigh ticks with no prompt from you: abandon, and the loop drops to #StandReadyLow.
    • While it still needs you, each tick plays ignored alone.
    • Clocked out: none of this counts.

    Always last, or alone

    • relay closes a reply that carries a baton for you to paste.
    • compact: auto-compaction, or a compaction prompt handed over, always the last sound.
    • low_health plays first, when an account is within 10% of a usage limit (every 30 minutes at most).
    • brakes is the only sound of a session under /brakes.
    • Muted (a Zoom meeting, the mute file, or clocked out): nothing plays and toasts are silent.

    Names: desktops and sessions

    These are the names chime uses: the virtual desktop names Windows keeps, and each session's tab title with the voice and words its name is spoken in. ▶ beside a voice plays that seat's name exactly as chime says it. New voice picks a random voice no other seat holds. Reset name drops a seat's own spoken words, so the title is read fresh.
